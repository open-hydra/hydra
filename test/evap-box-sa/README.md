# evap-box-sa

`evap-box-ta` (closed quiescent box, four streams of water droplets evaporating into N2, time accurate, MOSE + IGLOO)
with the gas solved as RANS: `[MOSE-Physics] equations = navier-stokes`, `turbulence = SA`, `schmidt = 0.7`.

With a turbulence model MOSE's primitive vector holds one variable more than the Euler run: the Spalart-Allmaras
variable `rhoNuTilde` (density times the modified eddy viscosity) after the energy slot, so `nprim = np + 1`. The coupler
(`src/app/MI2/MI2_Coupling.f90`, `IGLOOxMOSE`) adds the droplet sources to the species, momentum and energy slots of
MOSE's residual and must leave the slot after the energy alone. No other case of the repository runs a turbulence
model, so this is the only case where a coupler write past the energy slot can show.

## Faces and the wall distance

Every face is a symmetry plane (code 300), as in `evap-box-ta`. MOSE's viscous symmetry flux carries no species, heat
or SA flux and only the stress work of the tangential velocity, which vanishes on a mirror plane, so the two balances
the run prints (`closed-domain mass balance`, `closed-domain ENERGY bookkeeping`) are exact identities here as they are
in the Euler run. The domain has no wall face; MOSE then gives every cell its no-wall sentinel distance (1e8 m), the SA
destruction term and the wall part of the production vanish, and the SA variable evolves by vorticity production and
transport only. `[MOSE-Turbulence] point-implicit` (default true) is inert for the same reason. A no-slip wall would add
boundary layers and a wall thermal condition that the gate does not need.

## Initial condition

`[ICB-Block1] rhoNuTilde = 6.0e-5` is the SA variable ATLAS ICB writes as the 7th cell variable `rhoNuTilde` of
`INPUT/gas-ic.tec` (MOSE refuses an initial condition with fewer variables than `nprim`): three times the laminar
viscosity of N2 at 350 K, the far-field value the SA model recommends (`chi` about 3, eddy viscosity ratio `mut/mil`
about 0.21).

## Files

- `input.ini` — the case; `[GPB-*]`, `[BCB-*]`, `[ICB-*]` are read by ATLAS, the rest by the solvers.
- `MESH/mesh.tec` — 20x20x1 cells over 0.1 x 0.1 x 0.005 m (that of `evap-box-ta`).
- `INPUT/` — generated by ATLAS `34c128e` (gfortran build): `test/mi2-checks/regen_case.sh test/evap-box-sa <scratch> GPB BCB ICB`
  reproduces every file byte for byte. Against `evap-box-ta/INPUT/` only `gas-ic.tec` differs (the `rhoNuTilde` column).
- `check.py` — the checks of the gate, on a run log and its `OUTPUT/`.

## Run and gate

```
test/mi2-checks/run_case.sh test/evap-box-sa <scratch> 1 <log> <exe>      # 1 thread, about 8 s
test/mi2-checks/p13_rans_slot_check.sh [scratch-root] [exe]                # runs the case and check.py
```

`check.py <log> <OUTPUT> <INPUT>` prints PASS/FAIL lines and exits non-zero on a failure:

| check | bound | on the current sources (1 thread) |
|---|---|---|
| R1 mass balance, species 1 | rel.err <= 1e-12, injected >= 5e-9 kg | 2.213e-16, 7.476495e-9 kg |
| R2 energy bookkeeping | rel.err <= 1e-7, handed >= 1e-2 J | 2.281e-8, -0.1179 J |
| R3 SA variable (last field, iteration 1000) | finite, within 5 % of the initial field's everywhere; `mut/mil` in [0.1, 0.4] | -8.1e-4 .. +6.1e-3; 0.199 .. 0.210 |
| R4 log hygiene, RANS setup | exit 0, `GAME OVER`, no error markers, no `LK-interface:` warning; `Navier-Stokes`, `Turbulent, SA`; `rhoNuTilde` in the field | pass |

The 5 % bound of R3: the vorticity production moves the SA variable by at most `cb1 |omega| t` (below 0.5 % with the
0.02 m/s creep the evaporation drives), and the injected mass changes the density of an injection cell by at most 3 %;
a source leaking into the slot moves it by orders of magnitude within a few steps. The field is the last one MOSE writes
(`sol-diter = 50`; the run ends at iteration 1000: twenty coupling steps of 50 iterations).

The log carries IGLOO's `[WARNING] import_gas block 1: unbound gas variable(s): MIT` in the Euler run of `evap-box-ta`
as well; it is not a hygiene marker.
